BEFORE YOUR FIRST SERMON · QUICK GUIDE

How your little empire grows

Three things to know. No tutorial missions, just the rules you'll be using.

📣

Preach to earn

Plan your topic, delivery, movement and request for donations. As people pass, you'll see money and followers change. Make two decisions during the sermon. Afterward, discover which choices worked.

📚

Study instead of preaching

Reading books or watching parody video lessons takes the whole day. You gain experience in a skill, but earn no sermon money. Food and other daily costs are still deducted.

🚌

Survive, then move up

At the beginning, open Town → Street Life → Rummage Bins for one free chance to find a box, sign, clothing, food or coins each day. Once you have followers, getting spotted can cost you supporters. Bin searching disappears as you build a reputation or reach the city. After getting clean clothes and grooming, a travel offer appears when you qualify. Choose to move for $2.50 or wait seven days. Food and grooming cost more in bigger towns, but the crowds are bigger too.

Remember: Followers contribute again over time and some already believe in you, but poor grooming and aggressive sermons can lose them. Local wealth affects donations. Equipment never stacks in the same slot.

    HOW TO PLAY · v0.9.1

    Plan. Preach. React. Learn.

    Plan a day: Pick a subject, delivery, movement and fundraising approach. The predicted impact is deliberately hidden.

    Two live decisions: Every sermon pauses twice. Choose how to respond, with special options when you own a sign, donation cup or refreshments.

    Observe and learn: Your follower and cash totals change as the day progresses. Afterward, review the results and learn what your choices changed.

    Day and weather: Rain, crowds and weekdays matter. Sunday mornings are quiet on street corners but can suit an established congregation.

    Run a venue: At a hall or larger venue, choose which day and time your weekly main service runs. Other days are outreach.

    Survive and grow: Budget for meals and grooming. Early on, Town → Street Life → Rummage Bins offers free finds once a day. Once people follow you, being caught can lose followers and dedication; it disappears when you are established. When you qualify for the two $2.50 bus moves, a pop-up offers travel now or a seven-day reminder. The bigger towns have higher living costs as well as more visitors. Equipment does not stack in the same slot.

    Study days: In Town → Study & Media, spend a full day with a parody book or phone feed. Reading and streaming give experience instead of income. You still pay food, subscriptions and upkeep. Tap any skill for its exact gameplay role.

    Followers & funds: Recruits are harder to win, but regular, devoted and affluent supporters contribute repeatedly. Local wealth determines typical gifts, loyal supporters make a crowd seem convincing, and neglecting hygiene can drive people away.

    Endgame: Television advertising unlocks only in the megachurch. Buy your private jet to trigger a different divine reckoning for each god, then choose a permanent skill bonus and a different myth for your next life.
