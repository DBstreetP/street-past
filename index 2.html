HOW TO PLAY

Make each week count

1. Plan your weekly sermon. Pick a topic, delivery style, movement and how aggressively to ask for donations. A public mood appears every week. Choices combine, and repeating a strategy gets stale.

2. Watch the sermon. Pedestrians stop, chat, reject you, donate or join based on your preparation, skill, outfit and chosen deity.

3. Read your weekly report. See the money earned and spent, who joined or left, dedication, what worked, and experience gained. There is no automatic "skip week for riches" shortcut.

4. Visit Town. Once per week you may rummage through rubbish for free finds. The charity shop has changing stock. The high street offers better things. The parody online store needs premises.

5. Upgrade intelligently. Clothing, signs, platforms, amplifiers and furniture occupy individual equipment slots. Buying a new suit REPLACES the old outfit's bonuses; benefits never stack in the same slot. Books grant one-time experience. Old megaphones wear out. Staff are useful but take weekly wages.

6. Expand slowly. You need followers, cash, dedication and enough weeks in business to unlock the hall, church and finally megachurch. Overheads rise with size.
