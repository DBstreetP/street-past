BEFORE YOUR FIRST SERMON · QUICK GUIDE

How your little empire grows

Three things to know. No tutorial missions, just the rules you'll be using.

📣

Preach to earn

Plan your topic, delivery, movement and request for donations. As people pass, you'll see money and followers change. Make two decisions during the sermon. Afterward, discover which choices worked.

📚

Study instead of preaching

Reading books or watching parody video lessons takes the whole day. You gain experience in a skill, but earn no sermon money. Food and other daily costs are still deducted.

🚌

Survive, then move up

At the beginning, open Town → Street Life → Rummage Bins for one free chance to find a box, sign, clothing, food or coins each day. Once you have followers, getting spotted can cost you supporters. Bin searching disappears as you build a reputation or reach the city. After getting clean clothes and grooming, a travel offer appears when you qualify. Choose to move for $75 or wait seven days. Food and grooming cost more in bigger towns, but the crowds are bigger too.

Remember: Followers contribute again over time and some already believe in you, but poor grooming and aggressive sermons can lose them. Local wealth affects donations. Equipment never stacks in the same slot.

    Skills & field notes

    HOW TO PLAY · v0.9.6

    Plan. Preach. React. Learn.

    Plan a day: Pick a subject, delivery, movement and fundraising approach. The predicted impact is deliberately hidden.

    Two live decisions: Every sermon pauses twice. Choose how to respond, with special options when you own a sign, donation cup or refreshments.

    Observe and learn: Your follower and cash totals change as the day progresses. Afterward, review the results. The Notebook now has a Preaching Journal with past sermon plans, what actually happened, and a seven-day income ledger. It does not predict future results.

    Day and weather: Rain, crowds and weekdays matter. Sunday mornings are quiet on street corners but can suit an established congregation.

    Build a church: You must first travel from Cedar Flats through Ashbrook to New York City before property becomes available. The Organisation menu will appear when you reach the city. At a hall or larger venue, choose which day and time your weekly main service runs. Other days are outreach.

    Survive and grow: Budget for meals and grooming. Early on, Town → Street Life → Rummage Bins offers free finds once a day. You can search quickly for less risk or thoroughly for better finds. Food is a common reward. Being spotted becomes more likely as your followers grow; it disappears when you are established. When you qualify for the $75 first journey and $225 journey to New York, a pop-up offers travel now or a seven-day reminder. Each journey has a short bus cutscene. The bigger towns have higher living costs as well as more visitors. Equipment does not stack in the same slot. Early equipment is deliberately affordable; keep an eye on your next bus fare savings goal.

    Faster learning: Experience from preaching and study is now 65% higher. Study days: In Town → Study & Media, spend a full day with a parody book or phone feed. Reading and streaming develop your abilities faster than daily practice, at the cost of a day of preaching. You still pay food, subscriptions and upkeep. Tap the character icon for your branching skill trees. Combined skill levels unlock an ability point at each five-level milestone.

    Followers & funds: Recruits are harder to win, but regular, devoted and affluent supporters contribute repeatedly. Tap FOLLOWERS to see the groups and their individual donation totals. Local wealth determines typical gifts, loyal supporters make a crowd seem convincing, and neglecting hygiene can drive people away.

    Endgame: Television advertising unlocks only in the megachurch. Buy your private jet to trigger a different divine reckoning for each god, then choose a permanent skill bonus and a different myth for your next life.
