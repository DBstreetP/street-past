HOW TO PLAY · v0.3

Survive. Preach. Move. Build.

1. Plan each week: Choose your message, delivery, movement and donations. Predictions show stop chance, conversions, and being moved on.

2. Survive: Food costs £3 every sermon week. The soup kitchen reduces it to 75p. Wash, shave, and clean your clothes to look presentable. Ignore paid luxuries until you can afford them.

3. Travel twice: From Little Wold to Briarford, then to Grandford. Bus fare £2.50 per move. A clean shirt, washing and grooming are required; many local followers stay behind, but skills remain.

4. Learn from mistakes: Shouting and outrageous claims may get the sermon stopped early by authorities. Loyal crowds lower the risk. Experience levels rise with practice.

5. Explore Town: Scavenge, shop second-hand, then buy better kit. One item per slot; old gear never stacks. BlessBay unlocks after your first hall, which you can rent only in Grandford.

6. Build and begin again: Reach the megachurch to unlock Born Again. Start anew with a small permanent skill bonus and choose a new god if you like. Optional random events are off by default and can be ignored.
