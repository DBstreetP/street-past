THE RULES OF REVELATION

How to play

Each day, passers-by walk past your preacher. Some stop. Your charisma, knowledge and credibility affect whether they join. Followers have a dedication level that affects loyalty and donations.

Buy upgrades between days: clothing and signs get more people to stop; books improve your ability to keep them. Doughnuts and coffee bring people in, but can't make them believe.

Move up from the pavement to a community hall, then a church and eventually a megachurch. Larger venues cost money to maintain. Staff collect donations and improve attendance.

The game automatically saves to this browser after every purchase and day.
