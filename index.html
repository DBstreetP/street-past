    HOW TO PLAY · v1.3.1

    Three acts, one sermon

    1 · Opening: Choose a topic, then position four independent sliders for showmanship, controversy, personal connection and spirituality. Each displays its share of the total; only the slider you touch moves. Four full or four empty means 25% each.

    2 · Keep them hooked: Partway through each sermon, pause to adjust entertainment, personal stories, teaching and interaction independently. Their calculated percentages sum to 100%, and the live crowd reacts.

    3 · Closing: Use independent sliders for donations, new followers, return visits and loyalty. The total emphasis is always 100%. Most donations arrive at the closing appeal. Aggressive fundraising creates risk and may drive uncertain followers away.

    Understand your audience: Your Journal reveals patterns from your actual results. Weather, location, skills, equipment, follower commitment and rival campaigns all matter. You can reuse or adjust your last allocations.

    Manage your church: Follower totals include casual and regular supporters, not just donors. Buildings and expansions incur regular upkeep; donations and media pay the bills. Public scandals and rivals can reduce recruitment or collections for multiple days.

    Progress: On the first street, use You → Survival to clean up, find cheap supplies and eat. Travel by bus to Ashbrook and New York; rent your first hall before unlocking Office and Media Studio. Study for skill XP, recruit named characters and challenge rivals to debates. Equipment in the same slot never stacks.
